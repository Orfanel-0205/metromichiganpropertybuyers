Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

METRO MICHIGAN PROPERTY BUYERS ("we", "us", "our") operates this website to connect homeowners with a direct cash offer on their property. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this site, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, plus a small amount of technical data needed to operate the site securely.

  • Information you submit. When you request a cash offer we collect your name, email address, phone number, property address, property details (type, condition, bedrooms, bathrooms), your reason for selling, your timeframe, mortgage status, and any additional notes you provide.
  • Reviews. If you submit a testimonial we collect your name, location, rating, review text, and optionally your email address.
  • Technical data. We record the IP address and timestamp of form submissions to prevent abuse, and any campaign parameters (such as UTM tags) present in the link that brought you here.

How We Use Your Information

  • To evaluate your property and prepare a cash offer.
  • To contact you about your request by your preferred method — phone, email, or text.
  • To send you a confirmation that we received your submission.
  • To detect and prevent spam, fraud, and abuse of our forms.

We do not use your information to make automated decisions that produce legal effects, and we do not sell your personal information.

Text Messages (SMS)

We send text messages only if you explicitly opt in by checking the SMS consent box on our form. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, or by contacting us using the details below. Opting out of texts does not prevent us from contacting you by phone or email about a request you submitted.

How We Share Information

We share your information only where it is necessary to run our business:

  • Service providers. We use third parties to deliver email and text messages and to host our database. They may only process your data to provide those services to us.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We keep lead information for as long as needed to respond to your request and to meet our legal and record-keeping obligations. If you ask us to delete your information and we are not required to keep it, we will do so.

Security

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in an access-controlled database. Administrative access to lead data requires authentication. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices and Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
